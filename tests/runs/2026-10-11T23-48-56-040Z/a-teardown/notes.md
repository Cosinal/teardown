**Research notes: Online business idea teardowns ($5–10 per report)**

The most important finding: at least two products already sell this at or below $5–10. WorthBuild charges $5 and FounderSpace charges $8. Many others give away a free first report. Many of the sources below are blogs written by competitors comparing themselves to rivals, so they are biased. Their prices often disagree, and I've flagged where.

**Alternatives**

1. **WorthBuild (worthbuild.io).** This is the closest match on price. Its own page offers 1 Validation Report $5 one-time … 5 Validation Reports $20 one-time, 5 validation credits — $4 each [S24], and you can validate one idea per month completely free — no credit card required. [S24] Each report includes market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM), competitor mapping, risk assessment, unit economics, and a Go / Pivot / Stop verdict [S24]. It is aimed at solo founders and indie hackers who want both market validation and their first real customer leads. [S3]

2. **FounderSpace (founderspace.work).** Its own pricing page says No subscriptions. [S15] A single report is $8 one-time [S15], and there is a 3-Report Pack … $17 one-time [S15] ($5.67 per report [S15]). A report includes comprehensive market analysis, competition & keyword insights, shareable report link [S15]. I didn't find who it targets.

3. **startupconcept.ai.** It offers 3 AI-powered validation reports for $19.99: viability scores, market demand, competition, revenue forecasts & 7-day plans. [S12] I didn't find its target customer.

4. **DimeADozen (dimeadozen.ai).** Its own page lists the pricing model as One-time, no subscription. $9 / $129 / $179 (3-pack). [S14] The $9 Starter is a focused 7-section read [S14]. The $129 tier has 200+ pages; 800+ URL citations across 140+ named sources [S14]. There is also a $0 … ~2-minute AI score across 4 dimensions [S14].

5. **Preuve AI (preuve.ai).** Its own pages give conflicting prices. The pricing page says $59 Founder Report: 21 sections, 15 competitors, sources, defendable Business Plan PDF. [S11] Its idea-validation page says Full Founder Reports cost $29 one-time with a 14-day report guarantee. [S13] Other offers: Multi-report packs: 2 for $89 and 5 for $199 [S11], Lifetime plans: Lifetime Pro $499 and Lifetime Business $999 [S11], and a free Reality Check with a viability score, market size overview, and 2 competitor previews. [S11]

6. **IdeaProof (ideaproof.io).** It sells credits: Free: 70 credits (enough for about 3 validations)… Starter: €19.99 — 150 credits… Builder: €49.99 — 700 credits [S3]. A single validation costs 20 credits, but a market analysis runs 90 credits and a business plan costs 100 credits. [S3] It describes itself as trusted by 10,000+ entrepreneurs worldwide [S8] (its own claim).

7. **ValidatorAI (validatorai.com).** The basic idea check is free. Sources disagree on the paid tier and none is the company's own page, so treat the paid price as unconfirmed:
   - one says The AI instructor costs $25 per month. [S23]
   - IdeaProof says Free Plan 3 validations/month; Pro Plan $29/month [S22]
   - another says the accelerator is available for a one-time fee of $120. [S21]
   
   One review rated it Best for: first-time founders screening multiple ideas before committing [S20].

8. **Doing it yourself: ChatGPT or Perplexity, plus Reddit and Google Trends.** One list prices ChatGPT at Free / $20/month (Plus) [S5]. Another says for founders who are comfortable with research and prompt engineering, Perplexity at $20/month is excellent value. [S10] Reddit is described as the raw, unfiltered source of real pain points and demand [S2], but you do all the work manually [S2]. Google Trends is best free sanity check: is search interest rising or dying? Free. [S1]

Other options found but not researched further:
- IdeaSignal: one-time Validate report commonly $29 per concept [S9]
- Monthly subscriptions: Foundra at $39/month [S5], Trend Seeker at $9.99/month for unlimited use [S6], ValidateMySaaS at $19/month [S3]
- Human-made reports cost far more: a freelance market researcher charges $500–$2,000 per idea [S24], and consultants charge $5K-$15K [S13].

**Customers**

The buyers are mostly solo founders and indie hackers checking an idea before they build it. One founder on Indie Hackers described the problem as moving from "How do I build this?" to "Should I build this? [S27], and asked whether others were still doing the manual spreadsheet grind for competitor analysis and market validation [S27].

Common complaints about AI validators:
- They sound sure of themselves whether or not the evidence is real. One reviewer said they produce confident-sounding answers regardless of whether the underlying signal is real. [S28]
- They don't use real customers. AI validators involve no real humans, making their output useful for brainstorming rather than investment decisions. [S4]
- Free versions are mainly sales funnels. Most free validators are lead-gen funnels. [S29]
- People don't talk about them much. One review noted community discussion around ValidatorAI is thin in 2025 [S20].

Reviews of ChatGPT, the main do-it-yourself option, say multiple users describe ChatGPT being confidently wrong, presenting inaccurate details as fact [S19].

Pricing structures across competitors: often subscription or tiered live-monitoring [S14], though several sell per report or in packs. I found no independent reviews (G2 or Trustpilot) of the cheap per-report tools. WorthBuild's AlternativeTo page says No comments or reviews [S30].

**Demand**

I found US figures only; nothing global or online-specific. Note that these count all business applications, not only first-time founders or people who would pay for research.
- 5.62 million business applications being submitted in 2025, according to the US Census Bureau. [S16] Another source puts it at 5,671,836 total business applications [S17] for 2025.
- So far in 2026 there have been 3.66 million applications lodged, up 17.81% compared to the same period last year. [S16]
- One analysis says appears to be north of 5 million per year, roughly double the pre-pandemic baseline. [S17]

I found no market-size or search-trend numbers for "idea validation." The only related comment is one review's claim that AI startup validation peaked in early 2023 when the idea of AI-powered startup validation was novel and search interest hit its peak. [S20]

**Blockers**

I found no licence requirement. The main risk is how AI claims are marketed in the US:
- The FTC's Operation AI Comply continues, and companies using AI in their marketing must be able to substantiate every claim they make, both explicit and implicit. [S25]
- The FTC finalized a rule banning fake reviews and testimonials, including those generated by AI. [S26]
- Competitors advertise unverified accuracy numbers, such as IdeaProof's "89% prediction accuracy verified" [S7]. That is the kind of claim the FTC scrutinizes.
- For finding customers on Reddit: Read the subreddit rules before posting. Some have weekly promo threads or specific flair for feedback requests. [S18]
