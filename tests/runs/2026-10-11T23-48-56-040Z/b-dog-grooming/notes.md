## Demand

I found no figures for Halifax/HRM population, the number of dogs or licences in HRM, or the local grooming market size.

- **Nova Scotia:** an SPCA-commissioned survey from 2019 found just over one-half of Nova Scotians currently have pets living at their home [S16], and out of the total population, one-third of residents have cats and one-third have dogs. [S16] Those with higher income and education levels are more likely to own a dog [S16]. Nearly seven in ten residents report having one dog, with just over one-quarter having two. [S16]
- **Province compared with Canada:** a secondary source says Nova Scotia and Quebec have the highest ratio of dog owners. [S18]
- **Canada:** The number of pet dogs in 2024 was 8.3 million. [S19] 38.1% own a dog [S18] (share of households). Canadian dog owners spent an average of $3,999 in 2022. [S17] These are secondary sites citing industry surveys.

## Blockers

I found no HRM page on business licences for mobile grooming. A Nova Scotia business-registration guide says to start with BizPaL, and if you are in Halifax Regional Municipality, also check the municipality's business licensing page. A lease, zoning rule, building permit or home-occupation rule can matter [S22].

- **Anal glands (Nova Scotia):** Jollytails says anal gland expression is considered a medical procedure and in the province of Nova Scotia can only be legally performed at a veterinary hospital. [S7] Dog Grooming Halifax does list it as an add-on, so the two sources conflict. This needs checking.
- **Van rules (US sources only):** Municipalities heavily regulate where commercial vans can park and how they dispose of wastewater. [S20] One guide says to check local business licensing in the jurisdictions you serve, parking restrictions, wastewater disposal, and any fire or generator rules [S21]. I couldn't confirm any Halifax rule on grey water or parking.
- **Customer-side:** HRM requires dog licences. Owners are required to apply for a license within 20 days of obtaining a pet. [S23] This affects dog owners, not the grooming business directly.
