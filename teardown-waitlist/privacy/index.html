Who we are

Teardown is operated by Jorden Shaw, an individual based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described here. Contact: jorden@mail.grokbot.com.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we collect your email address, the date and time you signed up, a record that you consented, and the version of this policy you agreed to. We do not store your IP address, and we do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers on this site.

Why we collect it

We use your email only to tell you when Teardown launches, to deliver a complimentary teardown if you are among the first 10 signups, and to ask for feedback on the product. We will not use it for anything else without asking you first.

Legal basis

We process your information on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box on the form. You can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing does not affect anything we did before you withdrew.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share it only with the service providers that host this site and its database, as listed in our Data Policy, and only so they can provide that service. We may disclose information where the law requires it.

Emails

Every email we send will identify us, include our contact details, and contain a clear way to unsubscribe. We will act on unsubscribe requests within 10 business days.

Retention

We keep your information until you ask us to delete it or until the waitlist is no longer needed, whichever comes first. If the product does not launch, we will delete the waitlist within 12 months of the effective date of this policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you, and get a copy in a portable format;
  • correct it;
  • have it deleted;
  • object to or restrict how we use it, or withdraw your consent;
  • not be treated differently for exercising these rights.

To make a request, email jorden@mail.grokbot.com from the address you signed up with. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, to your local data protection supervisory authority if you are in the EU or UK, or to the California Privacy Protection Agency or your state attorney general if you are in the United States.

International transfers

Our service providers may store and process your information outside Canada, including in the United States. See the Data Policy for details.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the version and effective date above. If a change is material, we will email people on the waitlist before it takes effect.
