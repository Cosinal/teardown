**Subject:** A change to Teardown's privacy policy

Hi,

You're on the Teardown waitlist, so here's a short note about two changes to our privacy policy. The updated version (2026-10-12) takes effect on October 12, 2026.

**1. Site metrics.** We now use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to count visits and see how fast pages load. They set no cookies and don't record your IP address. They log the page you viewed, the site that sent you, your approximate location, your device and browser, and load times. We only see totals.

**2. AI processing of submitted ideas.** When you submit a business idea for a teardown, the idea and the details you enter with it are sent to Anthropic, the company whose AI models research the market and write the report. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, it doesn't train its models on that text and deletes it within 30 days, except where it has to keep it longer to enforce its usage policy or comply with the law.

Nothing changes about your waitlist signup. We still store only your email address, when you signed up and your consent, and we still use them only to tell you about the launch, send the free teardown if you're among the first 10, and ask for feedback.

The full policy is at https://teardown.ing/privacy (details in the Data Policy at https://teardown.ing/data). If you'd rather not stay on the list, reply to this email or use the unsubscribe link below and we'll delete your entry.
